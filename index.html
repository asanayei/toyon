How it works

Apple's Depth Pro model estimated how far away every pixel is, and the camera's field of view (about 12°, a zoom lens). That ran once, ahead of time. Your browser turns each of the 614,000 pixels into a point in 3D at its real distance, all on the GPU. At the start you see it from the original camera, so it looks just like the photo, until it turns.
